Problems solved · early awareness & accountability


FloodWatch brings fragmented rainfall, radar, weather, river-flow context and public water observations into one regional map. It helps users compare source-labelled signals, see when records are stale or missing, and find the official agency information needed to prepare earlier.


How earlier alerts could work: combine forecast rainfall, radar, upstream/downstream gauge trends, reservoir releases and official bulletins; check timestamps and missing data; apply locally validated thresholds; cross-check with IMD/CWC/state or district authorities; then send opt-in location-specific notices with evidence, valid time, uncertainty and an official source link. Human review and correction logs are essential.


Current status: live/API-dependent context and the water-observation explorer are implemented. FloodWatch does not yet issue validated local flood warnings or send emergency push/SMS alerts. Missing or stale data must never mean “safe”. Check NDMA SACHET official alerts ↗ · IMD ↗ · CWC ↗




Infrastructure & human-factor accountability


Flood impacts can also be influenced by reservoir operations, embankment condition, blocked drainage/culverts, land-use changes and warning/response timelines. The proposed evidence layer records source-linked facts and dates rather than assigning blame automatically.


    

  • Dams/reservoirs: published level, inflow/outflow or release bulletins, gate-status notices, latest public inspection date and responsible agency where documented.
    • 

  • Drainage & flood defences: official blockage, breach, erosion, inspection, maintenance and repair records.
    • 

  • Land use & response: dated floodplain/land-cover evidence and public warning, closure and evacuation timelines.
    • 



Dam-safety safeguard: FloodWatch must not guess “dam strength” from reservoir level or rainfall. Structural safety requires qualified engineering inspections and authorized instrumentation. If records are missing, show “not verified”; do not label a dam safe/unsafe or blame an operator without competent evidence.
